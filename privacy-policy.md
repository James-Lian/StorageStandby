# Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the local backup application (the "Software") handles your information. The Software is a local, full-stack application designed to help you manage your personal backups automatically across cloud storage providers including Google Drive, OneDrive, and Dropbox.

2. No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your personal data.
- Local Processing: All application logic, configurations, and operations run strictly on your local machine.
- No Remote Servers: We do not operate any external databases or remote servers that track your usage or capture your information.

3. Third-Party Cloud Integration (Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox)

The Software interacts directly with third-party cloud hosting services using their official APIs to manage your backups.
- Authentication: When you log into Google Drive, OneDrive, or Dropbox, the authentication process happens directly between your local machine and the respective provider using secure OAuth loopbacks.
- Access Tokens: Any access tokens or authentication credentials required to communicate with these cloud providers are stored locally on your own machine. We never have access to your passwords, cloud accounts, or access tokens.
- Data Transmission: Your backup files are transferred directly from your local machine to your chosen cloud provider. Your files never pass through any intermediary servers owned by us.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this document.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can open an issue or contact us via our official project repository page.
